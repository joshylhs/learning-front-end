Banana pancake

Healthy alternative whose taste is surprisingly close! (Makes 3-4 small pancakes)

Ingredients

  • 1 medium-large banana
  • 2 eggs
  • Pinch of salt
  • Maple syrup/honey + butter to garnish

Instructions

  1. Mash and whisk 1 medium-large banana with 2 eggs. Season with a pinch of salt.
  2. Pour batter onto a minimally-oiled pan on medium heat, making pankakes of desired size.
  3. Flip when bottom surface is browned and easy to unstick from the pan. (approx. 2-3 mins)
  4. Remove from pan when other side is browned. Serve with desired garnish!
